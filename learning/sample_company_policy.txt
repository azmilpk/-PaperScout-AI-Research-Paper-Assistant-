================================================================================
                     ACME CORP - ENTERPRISE POLICIES (2026)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: REMOTE WORK AND EQUIPMENT POLICY
Employees working remotely are eligible for a one-time home office setup stipend 
of $1,000. All hardware, including laptops and monitors, remains the property of 
Acme Corp and must be returned within 5 business days upon termination of employment.
VPN access is mandatory when accessing internal company code repositories and databases.

SECTION 2: VACATION AND PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. 
Unused PTO can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year.
Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted at least 
2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

SECTION 3: AI TOOL USAGE AND DATA PRIVACY
Employees are encouraged to utilize AI tools for productivity. However, pasting 
proprietary source code, customer personal data (PII), or unreleased financial metrics 
into public AI models is strictly prohibited. Approved internal AI tools must be used 
for any sensitive company tasks.

SECTION 4: HEALTH INSURANCE AND WELLNESS
Acme Corp provides 100% employer-covered health, dental, and vision insurance for 
all full-time staff. Employees also receive a $100 monthly wellness reimbursement 
usable for gym memberships, fitness trackers, or mental health app subscriptions.
